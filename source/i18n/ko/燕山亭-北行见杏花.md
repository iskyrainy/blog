### [Song] Zhao Ji

Cut and trimmed from icy silk, lightly layered in folds, faintly tinted with rouge evenly applied. A new-style lovely adornment, overflowing with beauty and melting fragrance, putting to shame the palace maids of the Ruizhu Palace. How easily they wither, and how many more—relentless winds and rains. Sorrow and bitterness—ask the desolate courtyard, how many times has spring passed into dusk?

By what means can I send this layered parting grief? These pair of swallows—how could they ever understand human speech? The sky is far, the earth remote, ten thousand rivers and a thousand mountains—who knows where his former palace lies? How could I not think of it? **Except in dreams, where sometimes I once returned. Without foundation—even the dreams have lately ceased to come.**

### [Song] Zhao Ji

Cut and trimmed from icy silk, lightly folded in many layers, faintly tinted with rouge evenly applied. In a new-style lovely adornment, her beauty overflows and her fragrance blends, putting to shame the palace maids of the Rui Zhu Palace. How easily they wither and fall, and how many more, merciless storms and rains. Sorrow and bitterness—ask the courtyard, how desolate it is, how many times has spring faded?

By what means can I send my parting grief, layer upon layer? These pair of swallows—how could they ever understand human speech? The sky is far, the earth remote, ten thousand rivers and a thousand mountains—who knows where his former palace lies? How could I not think of it? **Except in dreams, where sometimes I once went. Without basis—even dreams have lately ceased to come.**
